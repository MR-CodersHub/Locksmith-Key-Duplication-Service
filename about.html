Our Humble Origin

Built on Trust. Driven by Craft.

Keyora was born out of frustration with an industry plagued by opaque pricing, shoddy drill-and-replace tactics, and anonymous call centers.

In 2012, master locksmith Alistair Vance began with a single outfitted mobile van in the heart of the Metro District. His mission was straightforward yet rare: treat every lockout as an urgent human emergency, master the art of non-destructive lock manipulation, and quote clear, fixed prices before turning a single screw.

Today, Keyora has grown into a premier security consultancy and rapid-response network. We combine centuries-old artisan lock restoration with computerized laser key cutting and encrypted digital access controls — without ever losing the personal warmth and accountability of a local craftsman.

Our Core Values

Four unyielding pillars guide how we treat our clients, our tools, and the homes and businesses entrusted to our care.

Trust & Integrity

We operate with radical pricing transparency and background-vetted master locksmiths. We quote before we work, honor customer privacy, and never exploit distress.

Precision & Craft

Customer First
