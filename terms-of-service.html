1. Mandatory Verification for Lockout Assistance

To prevent unlawful entry and safeguard homeowners, businesses, and motorists, Keyora field technicians are legally mandated by the Master Locksmith Guild and state statutes to verify proof of occupancy or ownership before providing bypass access:

  • Residential Properties: Government photo ID matching the property address, current utility statement, or valid executed lease agreement.
  • Automotive Lockouts: Driver license and vehicle registration document or title matching the claimant's identity.
  • Commercial Facilities: Business authorization credentials, corporate building manager badge, or written tenant authorization.

Technicians reserve the right to decline service and contact law enforcement if identity credentials cannot be validated on-site.

2. Transparent Fixed Pricing Guarantee

Keyora rejects predatory "bait-and-switch" locksmithing practices. Our financial commitments to clients include:

  • Itemized Written Estimates: Before touching a tool to your lock, our master technician will inspect the hardware and provide a clear, itemized quote in writing.
  • Client Authorization: No work begins without explicit client signature or verbal authorization of the quote.
  • Flat Callout Rates: Standard mobile dispatch fees are stated upfront with zero surprise nighttime or holiday multipliers unless explicitly confirmed before driver dispatch.

3. 100% Non-Destructive Entry Commitment

We pledge to utilize non-destructive entry methods (precision lock-picking, decoders, bypass shims, and air wedges) as our primary mode of access.

In rare instances where a lock cylinder has suffered extreme mechanical jamming, vandalism, or internal seizure, drilling the cylinder core may become the sole physical remedy. In such cases, the technician will explicitly explain the mechanical necessity, provide the exact cost of the replacement cylinder, and obtain your consent before proceeding.

4. 1-Year Comprehensive Hardware & Labor Warranty

All new locks, deadbolts, commercial exit devices, and installation labor provided by Keyora are guaranteed under our comprehensive 1-Year Full Warranty:

  • Hardware Coverage: Any failure of springs, tumblers, deadbolt latches, or strike plates supplied by Keyora will be repaired or replaced free of charge.
  • Labor & Alignment: If a door shifts due to seasonal humidity within 365 days of installation, our technician will return to adjust strike plates at zero charge.
  • Exclusions: Warranty does not cover damage caused by burglary attempts, physical vandalism, client misuse, or unauthorized hardware tampering by third parties.

5. Master Key Systems & Restricted Keyway Protocols

Keys stamped with patented restricted keyway profiles (e.g., Medeco, Mul-T-Lock, ASSA Abloy) cannot be duplicated without the presentation of an official physical Security Signature Card signed by the primary account holder.

6. Emergency Dispatch Protocol & Cancellations

When an emergency mobile dispatch unit is en route, dispatch resources are committed immediately. Cancellations requested after a driver has been dispatched and is within 10 minutes of arrival may be subject to a nominal vehicle transit dispatch fee to offset fuel and driver mobilization.

7. Limitation of Liability

Keyora holds $2,000,000 comprehensive commercial liability insurance and bonding under license #LK-89412. Our liability for any claim arising from hardware installation or service is limited strictly to the direct repair or replacement cost of the affected hardware and door assembly.

8. Guild Arbitration & Contact

Any unresolved service inquiries are subject to neutral binding arbitration under the guidelines of the Master Locksmith Guild Dispute Resolution Committee.
