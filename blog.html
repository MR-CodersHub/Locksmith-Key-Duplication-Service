Field Wisdom

Small Habits.
Stronger Security.

Four straightforward, master-locksmith-approved habits you can implement this weekend without buying expensive hardware or undertaking major remodeling.

Keyora Guild Standard Practice
01

Never Hide Keys in Predictable Spots

Fake plastic rocks, doormats, and door frame ledges are the exact first places intruders search. Entrust a spare to a trusted neighbor or install an outdoor mechanical code vault.

02

Upgrade to 3-Inch Strike Plate Screws

Standard builder-grade screws are only 3/4 inch and catch weak trim casing. 3-inch hardened steel screws anchor directly into structural 2x4 wall studs, increasing kick-in resistance by 400%.

03

Use Dry Graphite, Never Petroleum Oil

Household spray lubricants attract airborne dust and road grime that turns into thick gunk inside the pin tumblers. Always use micro-fine dry graphite or dedicated PTFE lock fluid.

04

Rekey Cylinders When Moving In

Previous homeowners, property managers, cleaners, and contractors frequently retain unauthorized copies. Rekeying swaps the internal pins while preserving your existing decorative hardware.
