Motorist Services

Automotive Solutions We Deliver

Full diagnostics, key generation, and ignition cylinder repairs on-site.

Smart Key Fob Replacement

Generate OEM-grade push-to-start smart proximity keys. We sync the remote lock/unlock, trunk release, panic alarm, and passive entry sensors to your car's computer.

  • Push-to-start key fobs
  • Integrated emergency mechanical key blade cut

Laser Sidewinder Key Cutting

Most modern vehicles use high-security internal wavy-track keys. Our automated van-mounted CNC laser mills carve blanks with micrometer exactness.

  • High-security internal track profiling
  • Smooth insertion without ignition catching

Transponder Chip Programming

Even a mechanically cut key won't start the engine if the RFID transponder chip is unprogrammed. We code chips directly into your immobilizer and purge lost keys from memory.

  • Texas Crypto, Megamos & Philips chips
  • Old lost keys deleted for vehicle safety

Vehicle Lockout & Trunk Rescue

Locked your keys in the car or trunk? We use non-marring wedge bladders and coated bypass long-reach tools that open car doors without scratching paint, glass, or rubber weatherstripping.

  • 100% scratch-free guarantee
  • Double-locked deadlocked trunks opened

Broken Key Extraction

Keys often snap in the door cylinder or ignition during cold weather. We extract broken fragments cleanly using micro-spiral extractors without damaging internal wafers.

  • No ignition assembly replacement needed
  • Fresh replacement key cut from fragment

Ignition Repair & Rebuilding

If your ignition switch refuses to turn or gets jammed in ACC position (common on Honda, Ford, and GM vehicles), we rebuild the split wafers on-site to match your original key.

  • Keep your existing car key working
  • Save hundreds over dealer column replacement
