Why Automated Kiosks Fail & Guild Craftsmanship Succeeds.

Have you ever had a key copied at a big-box retail kiosk only to find it jams or has to be jiggled forcefully in the cylinder? That happens because automated kiosks copy the physical wear and rounding of your degraded original key.

At Keyora, we don't merely trace worn metal. Our digital optical profile reader reads the exact factory cut depths (root bitting) and recalibrates the cut to original manufacturer specifications. Your duplicate performs even smoother than the worn original key you brought in.

The Keyora Duplication Process

Four meticulous phases ensuring flawless mechanical insertion.

01

Optical Profiling

Your original key is scanned under high-resolution optical cameras to decode original factory bitting cuts.

02

Premium Blank Selection

We select high-tensile brass or nickel-silver blanks from genuine OEM keyway manufacturers (never cheap aluminum).

03

CNC Laser Milling

Automated rotary carbide cutters mill each tooth to exact depth angles, followed by automatic nylon-wheel micro-deburring.

04

Cylinder Gauge Testing

We test your duplicate key inside a calibrated lock cylinder plug to guarantee zero drag and 100% fluid rotation.
