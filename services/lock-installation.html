The Difference Is in the Execution.

A high-security lock is only as strong as its physical installation. Installing a deadbolt with uneven bore alignment or shallow 3/4-inch screws leaves your door vulnerable to a simple kick.

Keyora technicians use precision router jigs and razor-sharp hand chisels to carve mortise pockets without chipping fine hardwood finishes. We anchor heavy steel strike boxes with 3-inch case-hardened screws directly into the home's 2x4 framing studs, resisting over 800 lbs of kick force.

Installation Services

Hardware We Expertly Install & Replace

From new door prep to historic mortise restoration.

ANSI Grade 1 Deadbolt Fitting

Bore new cross-bore holes and install commercial-grade deadbolts engineered with hardened steel anti-saw pins, free-spinning cylinder collars, and 1-inch solid brass bolts.

  • Maximum residential security standard
  • Resistant to sledgehammer and drill attacks

Mortise Lock Installation

Chisel and route internal mortise pockets into luxury entry doors. Mortise locks combine a deadbolt and latch bolt into one heavy steel chassis for superior longevity.

  • Built for solid hardwood custom entrance doors
  • Smooth internal rocker switch operation

Smart Keypad Deadbolts

Upgrade from physical keys to touchscreen keypad or fingerprint locks. We enlarge strike recesses to guarantee the motorized bolt throws effortlessly without binding.

  • Compatible with Apple Home, Google & Alexa
  • Zero battery drain from misaligned latches

Heavy-Duty Strike Reinforcements

Standard builder strike plates use 3/4" screws anchored only into soft decorative pine trim. We install 12-gauge cold-rolled steel strike boxes anchored by 3" screws into the wall studs.

  • Prevents door jamb blowout from kicks
  • Flush aesthetic chisel finish

Multi-Point Locking Systems

Retrofit or replace European-style multi-point locks on 8-foot French doors and luxury patio sliders that lock at the top, center, and bottom simultaneously with one handle lift.

  • Maximum weather seal and draft prevention
  • Triple-point deadbolt security

Damaged Lock Replacement & Repair

Have you suffered an attempted burglary or a severely corroded exterior oceanfront lock? We replace broken parts, patch scarred jambs with brass wrap plates, and restore smooth locking.

  • Reinforcement wrap plates cover damage
  • Weather-resistant marine grade options
Artisanal Process

How We Install Your Locks

Clean, methodical craftsmanship from preparation to cleanup.

01

Door Geometry Check

We measure door thickness, backset (2-3/8" or 2-3/4"), bevel angle, and jamb alignment before drilling or chiseling.

02

Precision Boring & Mortise

Using clamped guide jigs and fine wood chisels, we cut crisp, splinter-free recesses that match your hardware's faceplate.

03

Deep Stud Anchoring

We mortise the strike plate into the jamb and drive 3-inch hardened steel screws straight into the wall stud framing.

04

Fluid Action Tuning

We cycle the deadbolt 20+ times, verifying zero latch rub, silent closure, and hand over your signed 1-year guarantee.

Lock Installation FAQs

ANSI (American National Standards Institute) tests locks for cycle longevity and forced entry resistance. Grade 1 is the highest commercial grade, tested to withstand 1 million cycles and 10 heavy hammer door blows. Grade 2 is light commercial / heavy residential, tested for 800,000 cycles. Grade 3 is basic builder grade, offering the least resistance to forced entry.
