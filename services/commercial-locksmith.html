Enterprise Solutions

Commercial Security Services Offered

Tailored hardware and architectural specifications for businesses of all scales.

Master Key Hierarchies

Multi-tiered master key systems allowing executives universal facility access, while managers and staff access only designated zones and storage rooms.

  • Grand Master, Sub-Master & Change Keys
  • Complete computerized keyway records

Panic Bars & Exit Devices

Heavy-duty push-pad exit devices for emergency egress doors. Ensure strict fire code compliance while keeping perimeter doors locked from exterior intrusion.

  • Rim, mortise and vertical rod panic devices
  • Alarmed exit bars for unauthorized door opening

Interchangeable Core (IC) Locks

Small Format (SFIC) and Large Format (LFIC) cores allowing instant 10-second cylinder replacement during staff turnover without taking door hardware apart.

  • Re-core locks in 10 seconds
  • Best, Medeco, and Schlage IC cylinder standards

Restricted Patent Key Control

High-security key blanks with active patents. No hardware store kiosk or local locksmith can duplicate these keys without written authorization from your facility signatory.

  • Guaranteed anti-duplication protection
  • Signed signature card verification

Commercial Door Closers

Grade 1 surface and concealed hydraulic door closers properly calibrated to meet ADA opening resistance thresholds (5 lbs max) and prevent door slamming.

  • Latch speed & sweep speed adjustment
  • Backcheck cushioning to protect hinges

Keyless Access & Electric Strikes

Integrate commercial keypad handles, RFID fobs, and electric strikes tied into your building intercom and security system.

  • Fail-safe and fail-secure wiring
  • Battery backup during power outages
