1. Scope & Core Principles

Keyora Locksmith & Security Solutions ("Keyora", "we", "us", or "our") provides high-security residential, commercial, automotive, and emergency locksmith services. We recognize that entrusting a service professional with access to your private residence, corporate office, or vehicle requires uncompromising trust.

We adhere strictly to the Ethical Fiduciary Standards of the Master Locksmith Guild. We never sell, monetize, rent, or disclose client names, addresses, access codes, or key bitting records to advertisers or unverified entities under any circumstances.

2. Information We Collect

To deliver certified locksmith and dispatch services, we collect only necessary logistical and technical data:

  • Identity & Ownership Verification: Full name, telephone number, email, and government-issued identification required legally before executing non-destructive lockout entries.
  • Property & Dispatch Location: Physical residential or business address, door location, or vehicle coordinate details.
  • Hardware & Lock Data: Make, model, cylinder type, backset dimensions, and keyway specifications (e.g. Schlage, Medeco, Mul-T-Lock, Yale).
  • Billing & Transaction Details: Itemized invoice histories, payment receipts, and written warranty certificates. Payment card numbers are processed via PCI-DSS Level 1 encrypted payment gateways and are never stored on our local servers.

3. Keyway & Bitting Code Confidentiality

Computerized key cutting and master key systems generate mathematical bitting depths (cuts from 0 through 9). Keyora safeguards this technical data with bank-grade precautions:

  • Restricted Keyways: Duplication records for patented keys are maintained in air-gapped, encrypted archives accessible only upon presentation of the client's physical Security Authorization Card.
  • Master Key Matrices: Grand master key schematics for corporate facilities are stored in multi-factor secured vaults with segregated access protocols.
  • Zero Casual Storage: Field technicians are prohibited from storing client bitting codes on personal smartphones or unencrypted portable media.

4. Emergency GPS & Dispatch Locations

When you initiate an urgent lockout request via phone or our digital booking modal, our mobile dispatch system utilizes temporary geolocation to route the closest workshop van. Once the service call is closed and the invoice is stamped, live driver GPS tracking data is purged automatically.

5. Non-Disclosure & Third Parties

Keyora does not share client information with external marketers. Third-party disclosures are limited strictly to:

  • OEM Hardware Manufacturers: Solely when registering official product warranties or factory key replacement authorizations on high-security Medeco or ASSA Abloy cylinders.
  • Law Enforcement Compliance: Solely in compliance with valid judicial warrants or mandatory state licensing audits under licensed locksmith statutes.

6. Hardware Warranty Retention

Service logs, invoice copies, and hardware warranty registrations are retained for 36 months to guarantee that our 1-Year Full Warranty and ongoing alignment guarantees can be validated and serviced without delay.

7. Client Access & Rights

Commercial and residential clients hold the right to request a complete copy of their property hardware registry, update authorized signatories on restricted keyway accounts, or request the secure deletion of non-regulatory historical logs by contacting our compliance office.
