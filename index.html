Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know regarding response protocols, damage-free entry guarantees, rekeying versus lock replacement, and key duplication.

No. At Keyora, our master locksmiths practice non-destructive entry as our standard operating procedure. Using precision decoders, tension wrenches, and specialized picks, we manipulate the tumbler pins to open the door without drilling or harming the lock, cylinder, weatherstripping, or door frame. Drilling is strictly a last-resort measure used only if the internal lock cylinder has suffered physical mechanical seizure.
Rekeying retains your existing deadbolt and handle hardware while replacing only the internal bottom pins inside the cylinder. This renders any old keys completely useless and creates brand-new working keys at a fraction of the cost. Lock replacement involves removing the entire hardware housing and installing a brand-new mechanical or electronic lockset.
For standard commercial and residential keys bearing decorative "Do Not Duplicate" stamps, there are no legal restrictions and we can duplicate them immediately. For patented restricted keyways (such as Medeco, Schlage Primus, or Mul-T-Lock), we require authorization from the registered property administrator or security card holder to protect key control integrity.
